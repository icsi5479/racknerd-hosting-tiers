## Web hosting plans FAQ

### Is cPanel really included, or is it an add-on?

It's included on all four tiers. That matters more than it sounds: budget hosts increasingly charge separately for cPanel licenses or substitute a homegrown panel, so a $2.49 plan with full cPanel is rarer than it looks. The panel also gives you one-click WordPress installs and on-demand backups whenever you want them.

### Is unmetered bandwidth actually unlimited?

There's no per-gigabyte meter, but shared accounts still live inside fair-use limits on CPU and memory. A normal blog or business site will never approach those ceilings. A video-streaming or file-download project will, and should be on a VPS anyway.

### Can I upgrade to a bigger plan later?

Yes. Upgrades are a support-ticket request, and your files, databases and email stay in place. Don't order a second plan to "add" sites — that's the mistake that doubles your bill for no reason.

### Does RackNerd hosting include email?

You can create mailboxes on your own domain through cPanel on every tier. Two cautions: mailbox storage counts against your plan's disk space, and it's worth confirming the account allowance for your tier before committing a whole team to it.

## Before you click order

Three checks worth doing, in order:

1. **Read the refund terms first.** Budget hosts differ sharply here — some offer short cash-refund windows, some issue account credit instead. If there's any chance you'll change your mind, learn the rule before paying rather than after.
2. **Have a domain ready.** RackNerd doesn't bundle one. Register it wherever you like (roughly $10–15/yr for a .com) and point its nameservers at your new account after setup. Keeping the domain separate from your host is also the safer long-term arrangement.
3. **Set up your own backup schedule from day one.** Regardless of which host you pick, an off-server backup is the one habit every site owner eventually thanks themselves for.
